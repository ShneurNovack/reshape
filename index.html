Questions, answered honestly.

How long does a typical engagement run?+
Most identity engagements run five weeks end-to-end. Product work and embedded direction usually extends to 8–14 weeks; we'll scope honestly on the kickoff call.
What does it cost to work with you?+
Identity sprints start at $48,000. Product engagements start at $96,000. We don't haggle, but we'll happily reshape scope to fit the right number for both of us.
Do you work with early-stage teams?+
Often. We hold one founder slot per cohort at a reduced rate — apply by the form below and tell us your story.
Where is the studio based?+
Brooklyn, Lisbon, and Tokyo, in that order of timezone. We work remotely with frequent in-person workshops; we'll fly out for the kickoff.
