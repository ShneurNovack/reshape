  • $100 becomes $108, six times chai. $250 becomes $252. One tap, no typing.
  • Every chai amount is labeled on the buttons and under the total, so the meaning is right there.
  • Only when it helps. It never pushes a donor more than a small step up.

It remembers your donors.

Giving again should take seconds. GiveKit keeps a returning donor's details on their own device, and picks up a half-finished gift right where they left it.

  • Welcome back, Sarah. Returning donors are greeted by name, with a one-tap repeat of their last gift.
  • Already filled in. Name, email, phone and address are ready, so all they add is payment.
  • Pick up where you left off. Everything they type is saved as they go. Got interrupted? Next visit, one tap finishes the gift.
  • Private by design. Saved only in the donor's own browser, with a Not Sarah? Start fresh link. Card details are never saved.

Questions

Is GiveKit really free?

Yes, during the beta. And Chabad houses that join the beta keep GiveKit free after we start charging. In return, we'd love to hear about any bugs and ideas.

How does setup work?

You give us developer access to your ChabadOne site, and we install GiveKit for you, usually the same day. We set your colors, amounts and links, and test the whole flow on your site before anyone sees it.

Do I need a new payment processor or account?

No. Gifts go through your existing ChabadOne donate form and processor, exactly as they do now. GiveKit only changes how the page looks and guides the donor.

Will my donations still show up in ChabadOne?

Yes. Every gift is submitted by ChabadOne's own form, so receipts, reports and donor records are the same as today. Add-ons are added to the gift and noted on the record.

What happens if ChabadOne updates their template?

We keep an eye on it and fix anything that changes, usually before you'd notice. And if GiveKit ever fails to load, your page simply shows the standard ChabadOne form, so donations never stop.

Is it safe? Do you see card numbers?

Card details are typed into ChabadOne's own fields and go straight to their secure checkout. GiveKit never stores or sends card information anywhere. The only things remembered are a returning donor's name and contact details, on their own device, so the form can fill itself in next time.

Does it work with PayPal and donor advised funds?

Yes. Every payment option on your current page keeps working, including PayPal, checks and donor advised funds.

Can I change amounts, colors or add-ons later?

Anytime, in your GiveKit dashboard. Or just tell us and we'll do it.

What if I want to stop?

Tell us and we'll remove it. Your donate page goes straight back to the standard ChabadOne page. No contracts.
